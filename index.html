Design meets the table

A kitchen showroom you can experience.

Unravel Cucine brings food, kitchen design, and cooking together under one roof. Imagine choosing cabinetry while seeing a kitchen in action, learning from a chef, and sharing something freshly made. The concept is being developed around the simple pleasure of gathering in the kitchen.

Three parts of one place

Explore. Learn. Taste.
